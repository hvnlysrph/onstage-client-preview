RECREATIONAL DANCE

RECREATIONAL CLASSES

On Stage offers recreational dance classes that introduce children to movement, music, dance technique, and performance in an age-appropriate environment.

SING. DANCE. ACT. PERFORM.

Students can explore Broadway jazz, hip hop, ballet, and tap while building confidence and learning skills they can use on stage.

DANCE CLASS OFFERINGS

THEATER JAZZ I & II

A recreational Broadway jazz class combined with acting for the stage. Students learn basic dance movement and acting skills that will be used in their Student Showcase performance.

HIPPITY HOP

An age-appropriate beginner hip hop class for students ages 5–7. This class gives young dancers a welcoming place to explore movement and music.

STUDENT SHOWCASE

Theater Jazz students apply the movement and acting skills learned in class during a Student Showcase performance featuring music from a Broadway musical.
