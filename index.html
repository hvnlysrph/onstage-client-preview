Explore Our Programs

We offer programs for students of all ages and abilities.

MUSICAL THEATER / ACTING

Here, students have the incredible opportunity to learn how to sing, dance, act, and perform. Our dedicated instructors are committed to nurturing talent and fostering creativity in a fun and supportive environment. Join us and unleash your passion for the performing arts.

Our theater and acting programs run for a full 12 months throughout the year.

FIND OUT MORE

VOICE AND PIANO

We offer engaging voice and piano lessons designed specifically for children who are eager to enhance their musical theater education. Our experienced instructors create a fun and supportive environment, helping young performers develop their skills and confidence. Join us and let your child's musical journey begin!

Voice lessons run September - June each year.

FIND OUT MORE

DANCE

On Stage, offers a comprehensive dance program for ages 3 to 18. Our classes are led by trained professional teachers who are dedicated to nurturing each student's passion for dance. Join us to explore various styles and develop your skills in a supportive and inspiring environment. Our dance programs run for a full 12 months throughout the year.

FIND OUT MORE

SUMMER PROGRAMS

Join us this summer for exciting classes at On Stage! Whether you're a beginner or an experienced performer, we offer a variety of programs designed to enhance your skills and boost your confidence. Dive into the world of acting, dancing,and music. Make unforgettable memories with fellow creative's. Sign up today and let your talent shine.
