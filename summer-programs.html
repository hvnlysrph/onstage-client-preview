SUMMER 2026 PROGRAMS

Sing, dance, act, perform, and build confidence this summer at On Stage.

MAKE THIS SUMMER COUNT

On Stage offers summer classes for beginners and experienced performers. Students can strengthen their skills in musical theater, ballet, pointe, contemporary, jazz, tap, modern dance, and performance.

Programs are designed to help students improve technique, build confidence, work with fellow performers, and enjoy a creative summer experience.

Register Now Call the Front Office

FEATURED SUMMER PROGRAMS

American Girl Doll Story Book Ballet

A story-based introductory ballet class for young dancers. Students use imagination, movement, and American Girl stories to bring dance adventures to life.

Ages: 3–4

Schedule: Monday or Wednesday, 4:00–4:30 p.m.

Children's Summer Musical Theater

Students sing, dance, act, and perform songs from the all-American music of George M. Cohan.

Ages: 7–10

Dates: July 7–August 19

Schedule: Tuesdays, 5:30–7:00 p.m.

Tuition: $150 for the six-week program

Teen Summer Musical Theater

Teen performers develop their singing, acting, dancing, and performance skills using songs from West Side Story.

Ages: 11–15

Dates: July 7–August 19

Schedule: Tuesdays, 5:30–7:30 p.m.

Tuition: $150 for the six-week program

Summer Dance Classes

Summer training includes classical ballet, pointe, contemporary, classic jazz, tap, and modern dance.

Students can improve technique, strength, musicality, precision, control, flexibility, and artistry.
